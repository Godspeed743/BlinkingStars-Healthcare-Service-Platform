Specialized Audiology Tests

Our comprehensive range of hearing assessments helps identify and address auditory issues in children.

Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA)

Measures hearing sensitivity across different frequencies to determine the type and degree of hearing loss. This test helps identify the softest sounds a child can hear at various pitches.

Benefits:

  • Identifies specific frequency hearing loss
  • Helps determine appropriate interventions
  • Monitors hearing changes over time

Impedance Audiometry

Evaluates middle ear function by measuring how well sound energy transfers through the eardrum and middle ear. This test helps identify fluid buildup or other middle ear issues.

Benefits:

  • Detects middle ear disorders
  • Identifies eardrum perforations
  • Evaluates Eustachian tube function

Brainstem Evoked Response Audiometry (BERA)

Measures brain wave activity in response to sound, helping to evaluate the auditory pathway from the ear to the brain. Particularly useful for infants and young children.

Benefits:

  • Detects hearing loss in infants
  • Identifies auditory nerve disorders
  • Evaluates brainstem function

Otoacoustic Emissions (OAE)

Measures sounds produced by the inner ear in response to stimulation, helping to assess cochlear function. This test is often used for newborn hearing screening.

Benefits:

  • Non-invasive screening method
  • Detects inner ear dysfunction
  • Ideal for newborn screening

Speech Audiometry

Evaluates a child's ability to hear and understand speech at different volumes. This test helps determine how hearing loss affects communication abilities.

Benefits:

  • Assesses speech recognition
  • Helps with hearing aid fitting
  • Evaluates functional hearing ability
