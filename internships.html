Internship Opportunities

Join our dedicated team and make a positive impact in the lives of children. Our internship program offers hands-on experience in a supportive environment.

Speech and Language Therapy Internship Program

Enhance Communication Skills Through Real-World Practice Step into the world of speech and language therapy with our hands-on internship. Learn how to support children in improving their communication abilities while gaining real clinical experience in a supportive, child-friendly environment.

Holistic Child Development Internship

Join a Multidisciplinary Journey in Child Development Be a part of an integrated developmental care center where you’ll learn to work across disciplines—speech, occupational, behavioral therapies—to help children grow and thrive holistically. Great for psychology, special education, and early intervention learners.

Occupational Therapy Internship Program

Hands-On Experience in Pediatric Occupational Therapy Support children in developing essential life and motor skills. This internship provides exposure to therapeutic methods that enhance independence in daily living. Suitable for OT students seeking practical exposure.

Autism Assistant Course

Practical Training to Support Children with Autism This beginner-friendly course equips you with techniques to assist children on the autism spectrum in everyday settings. Perfect for caregivers, teachers, and aspiring therapists. No prior experience needed.

Pediatric Physiotherapy Internship Program

Empowering Children Through Movement and Play Learn how physiotherapy enhances mobility, strength, and coordination in children. This internship offers direct experience with pediatric rehab techniques under expert guidance. Tailored for physiotherapy students passionate about pediatrics.
