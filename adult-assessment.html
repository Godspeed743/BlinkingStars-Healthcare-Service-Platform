Adult Assessments

Assessment is the first step toward the right intervention. Standardized assessments provide valuable information that helps our clinical team understand a person's needs and make or rule out a diagnosis.

Schedule Assessment Get More Information

PSYCHODIAGNOSTIC ASSESSMENTS

Psychodiagnostic assessments help us understand a person’s personality, behavior patterns, and emotional concerns. Using a set of standardized tests, our clinicians identify the nature and severity of difficulties along with related factors, based on the individual’s needs.

COGNITIVE AND NEUROPSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENTS

Neuropsychological assessments help identify and measure difficulties in thinking, memory, or behavior, guiding focused intervention planning. Standardized tests, including IQ assessments when needed, are used for accurate results.

PROCESS ASSESSMENTS

Process assessments help us understand both the severity and specific nature of a client’s concerns, allowing therapists to tailor therapy to individual needs.

PERSONALITY ASSESSMENTS

Personality assessments help identify traits that may underlie a client’s difficulties. Understanding these patterns allows us to address challenges that affect personal, social, and occupational functioning.
