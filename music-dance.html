Healing Through Rhythm and Movement

At Blinking Stars, we believe in the transformative power of music and dance therapy. Our specialized programs combine evidence-based therapeutic techniques with creative expression to help children with special needs develop essential skills while having fun.

Music Therapy

Our music therapy program uses musical interventions to address physical, emotional, cognitive, and social needs. Through singing, playing instruments, and rhythmic activities, children develop communication skills, improve motor coordination, and express emotions in a safe, supportive environment.

  • Enhances communication and language development
  • Improves fine and gross motor skills
  • Reduces anxiety and promotes emotional regulation
  • Builds social interaction and turn-taking skills

Dance Therapy

Dance and movement therapy harnesses the connection between body and mind. Through structured movement activities, creative expression, and rhythmic patterns, children improve physical strength, balance, coordination, and body awareness while building confidence and self-esteem.

  • Develops balance, coordination, and spatial awareness
  • Strengthens core muscles and improves posture
  • Enhances body awareness and proprioception
  • Promotes emotional expression and stress relief

Our Programs

We offer a variety of music and dance therapy programs tailored to meet individual needs and goals.

Individual Music Sessions

One-on-one music therapy sessions designed specifically for your child's unique needs and goals.

  • Personalized treatment plan
  • 45-60 minute sessions
  • Progress tracking

Group Music Therapy

Small group sessions that promote social interaction, peer learning, and collaborative music-making.

  • 3-5 children per group
  • Social skill development
  • Peer interaction opportunities

Dance Movement Therapy

Structured movement sessions that combine dance, yoga, and creative movement to enhance physical and emotional well-being.

  • Motor skill development
  • Body awareness training
  • Emotional expression

Home-Based Sessions

Convenient in-home music and dance therapy sessions in the comfort of your child's familiar environment.

  • Comfortable setting
  • Family involvement
  • Flexible scheduling

Online Sessions

Virtual music and dance therapy sessions via video conferencing for families who prefer remote access.

  • Accessible from anywhere
  • Interactive virtual tools
  • Flexible timing

Workshops & Events

Special workshops, recitals, and community events that celebrate progress and build confidence.

  • Performance opportunities
  • Community building
  • Family participation

Benefits of Music & Dance Therapy

Discover how music and dance therapy can positively impact your child's development and well-being.

Communication

Enhances verbal and non-verbal communication skills through musical expression

Motor Skills

Improves coordination, balance, and fine/gross motor abilities

Emotional Well-being

Promotes emotional expression, reduces anxiety, and builds self-confidence

Social Skills

Develops social interaction, turn-taking, and collaborative skills

Cognitive Development

Enhances memory, attention, and problem-solving abilities

Sensory Processing

Helps regulate sensory responses and improve sensory integration

Focus & Attention

Improves concentration and sustained attention through engaging activities

Self-Expression

Provides creative outlets for expressing thoughts, feelings, and experiences

Who Can Benefit?

Music and dance therapy can be beneficial for children with various special needs and conditions.

Autism Spectrum Disorder

Enhances communication, social skills, and emotional regulation

Developmental Delays

Supports overall development and milestone achievement

Speech & Language Disorders

Improves articulation, vocabulary, and communication skills

Motor Impairments

Enhances coordination, balance, and motor planning

ADHD

Improves focus, impulse control, and self-regulation

Emotional & Behavioral Challenges

Provides healthy outlets for expression and emotional regulation
